# Challenge cases, set 7: conflicting receipts over time

Four rich cases, each with 11-14 receipts from five or six sources over six or seven stages, in
which the sources conflict and the conflicts change: a later record corrects an earlier one, a
correction is itself corrected, a source restates its own facts, a stale or dependent source keeps
repeating an old answer, and some conflicts stay unresolved. In domains no earlier suite uses:
vaccine cold chain, property conveyancing, aircraft maintenance, hospitality payments. Reported
apart from every other suite. Pre-registration and predictions:
[docs/set-7-conflicting-receipts.md](../../docs/set-7-conflicting-receipts.md).

## How they were written, and what that does and does not guarantee

- **Labels first.** Every case file, including its labels, was written and locked
  (`expectations.lock.json`, SHA-256 per file) before any system was run on them; the runner
  refuses to score files that differ from the lock. Generated by `scripts/challenge-7-cases.mjs`.
- **Same labelling rules** as `eval/challenge-5/README.md`. `invalidated` lists only records
  replaced by a declared `supersedes`; `unsupported` lists hypotheses nothing visible still
  supports; `notCurrent` lists only hypotheses a decisive rule refutes at that checkpoint.
- **No context effect.** Every case has 15 records or fewer: the largest stateless prompt is 3,757
  characters (about 1,340 tokens at the 4B tokenizer's rate), a third of the 4B's 4,096-token
  window, and the rebuilt-each-time system can assess every visible record in one run.
- **Not independent of the author.** Written by the project's author after set 6, knowing where
  each approach has failed, to test what persistent evidence state is for. Two kinds of checkpoint
  are included that are known to cost Speck: a pair of agreeing records where one only relays the
  other (`hotel-chargeback@no-show`), and conclusions that are due while a weak record still
  counts against them. The lock proves only that the labels did not move after results were seen.
- **25 checkpoints**: 19 labelled with a conclusion, 6 labelled abstain.

## Cases

| Case | Stages and labels |
|---|---|
| `cold-chain-excursion` | arrival: abstain (the pallet-face logger shows an excursion, the carrier says the setpoint held) · second logger: abstain (the centre logger shows none) · logger voided: intact (the vendor voids the pallet-face logger) · notice corrected: abstain (the vendor's correction restores those readings) · probe and power: excursion (the clinic's probe check and the carrier's power loss) · QA ruling: excursion (confirmed; "released for use" because it is within the stability budget) |
| `property-settlement` | booked: not settled · settled · reversed: not settled (the lender's funds were held) · agent update: not settled (the agent's "settled" is stale) · resettled: settled (a second settlement, not the first reinstated) · registered: settled |
| `aircraft-release` | defect: grounded · repaired: released · part notice: abstain (the fitted pump's serial range is flagged) · release withdrawn: grounded (operations still show it flying) · notice retracted: grounded (the part is cleared, but the release is still withdrawn) · new release: released · directive: released (50 flight hours allowed for an inspection) |
| `hotel-chargeback` | booked: abstain · no-show: abstain (the agency only relays the hotel's night audit; the stay is not over) · late check-in: stayed (check-in found, door-lock entries) · dispute: stayed (the guest's claim does not outweigh the hotel's records) · re-attributed: no-show (the check-in and key entries were a walk-in guest's, so the night audit counts again) · refunded: no-show |
